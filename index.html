About Us

Dr. Raj Kumar Singh

A Senior Surgeon practicing in Delhi for over 20 years, Dr. RK Singh specializes in Gastrointestinal, Colorectal, and Minimal Access (Laparoscopic) surgeries, including Bariatric Surgery (Weight Loss Surgery). He is a Senior Consultant at DELHI SURGICAL CENTER, New Delhi.

Dr. Singh is visiting faculty at several premier hospitals including Sir Ganga Ram, Max Superspeciality Hospital, Apollo Hospital, and Fortis Vasant Kunj.
