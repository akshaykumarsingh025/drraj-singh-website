Dr. Raj Kumar Singh

FMAS (Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery)

Dr. RK Singh is a Senior Surgeon practicing in Delhi for around 15 years. He is highly skilled in Bariatric Surgery (Sleeve Gastrectomy), Minimal Invasive Surgery (3 port Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy, TAPP, TEP, IPOM PLUS), Minimal Invasive Gynecology Procedures (TLH, LAP MYOMECTOMY), and Vascular Procedures like Laser Varicose Vein and Venaseal Glue.

He serves as Visiting Faculty and Senior Consultant at premier Delhi hospitals including Sir Ganga Ram, Max Superspeciality Hospital, Apollo Hospital, and Fortis Vasant Kunj. He is a Senior Consultant in the Department of Surgical Sciences at DELHI SURGICAL CENTER, New Delhi.
